A Sleepy Dream

I remember a place where the snow was white and soft, like a giant blanket. Then, the weather changed, and blue raindrops began to fall, splashing gently into a great fountain. I floated through the plaza, feeling light and warm despite the rain. The whole world was just blue and white, and it felt like the most peaceful nap I've ever had.
